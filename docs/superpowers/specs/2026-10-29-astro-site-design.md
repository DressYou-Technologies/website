# Final site on Astro — design

## Intent

Replace the coming-soon page at dressyouapp.com with the final site the founder designed in Claude Design
(`ui_kits/site/DressYou Site.dc.html` in the design-system export, built from the preview-2 spike). The site
has to read as a real product site to visitors and to reviewers such as AWS Activate. Every person and garment
on it is synthetic.

## Goals

- A faithful port of the design: section order, copy, layout at desktop and phone widths, and its GSAP motion
  (hero wall drift and parallax, the phone's try-on story, the dark "more than a try-on" band, the pinned
  stylist story with snapping, the finale).
- The 42 generated images replace every temporary image, served as responsive AVIF/WebP at the sizes each
  slot shows, with width and height set so nothing shifts while loading.
- Maintainable: one component per section, and the people, looks and garments listed once in a data file.
- Fast: no render-blocking third-party script, fonts self-hosted, images lazy below the fold. Target: mobile
  Lighthouse performance ≥ 90 and accessibility ≥ 95 on the Vercel preview.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` shows every section still and complete.

## Non-goals

- No sign-up, waitlist or account flow on the page.
- English only (release focus since 2026-10-26). The design's language switch has no French copy behind it, so
  it is left out. *My call.*
- No new sections or copy beyond the design, except the AI-imagery line below. The feed tile keeps its copy
  with no caption: the generated images are temporary (founder, 2026-10-29).

## Anti-goals

- Shipping images of real people, shop photos or anything with a third-party logo.
- A page that looks finished but whose buttons lead nowhere a reviewer can follow.

## Constraints

- Vercel deploys `main` to production; every PR gets a preview. Work stays on `feat/astro-site` until the
  founder approves the preview.
- PR title rule and CI stay. CI gains `npm ci && npm run build` before the tests.
- Copy follows AGENTS.md §7 of the product repo: the body is never the subject of a negative sentence; the
  render never answers the fit question.
- The design system marks generated imagery as labelled. Footer line: "People shown on this page are
  AI-generated." *My call, founder told; removable.*
- The metadata set (`index.html` head, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, JSON-LD) stays consistent; it moves to
  `src/` and `public/` but the rule holds.
- The product repo's hosting plan (#229) moves the site to S3 + CloudFront later; Astro's static output works
  on both.

## Approaches

1. **Astro + GSAP (picked by the founder, 2026-10-29).** Static output to `dist/`, components, built-in image
   pipeline (sharp), GSAP from npm, scoped CSS on the design-system tokens. Costs a build step and
   `node_modules`; lifts the repo's no-dependency rule.
2. Build-free, GSAP files copied into `dist/`, image variants pre-generated by a script. Keeps the rule, but 27
   wall tiles and dozens of `srcset`s are hand-written markup.
3. Vite + plain HTML. Bundling without components or an image pipeline: the cost of a build without its main
   benefits.

Tailwind is left out: the design's sizes are `clamp()` expressions that become unreadable utility classes.
Motion is left out: it does what GSAP already does.

## Decided

The founder picked Astro + GSAP; the rest of this list is my call.

- Astro, static output, TypeScript strict, no UI framework (sections are `.astro` components; motion is plain
  TS modules per section, started from one entry).
- GSAP and ScrollTrigger from npm (3.15 today; the export loads 3.13 from a CDN), versions pinned by the lockfile. The QR code beside "Get the app" uses `qrcode-generator` at build time
  into an inline SVG, so no runtime library.
- Design tokens copied from the design system's `tokens/*.css` (colours, type, spacing, motion) into
  `src/styles/tokens.css`. Manrope and IBM Plex Mono self-hosted, latin subsets from the export.
- Image masters: the generated JPEGs, resized once to twice their largest display size, live in
  `src/assets/people/`, `src/assets/garments/`, `src/assets/feed/`, beside `manifest.json` (model, prompt,
  date for every image). Astro derives every served size and format.
- Tests: Node's runner over the built `dist/`: metadata consistency, every image has width, height and alt,
  every local reference exists, no external script origins.

## Calls from the builder check (mine)

- Links with no destination are hidden until they have one: the menu's About, Journal, Careers, Press and
  Help, the three social icons, and the footer's About, Careers and Press. In-page anchors stay.
- Legal notice, privacy and terms become real pages (`/legal`, `/privacy`, `/terms`) built from what is
  known (DressYou Technologies, 47 rue Vivienne, 75002 Paris, info@dressyouapp.com, host Vercel); the
  missing legal fields are the founder's, and the production merge waits for them.
- The metadata set moves to the new hero line, "Try it on before it's yours.", with a description from the
  hero paragraph, and `og-image.jpg` is regenerated from the wall images.
- Slot mapping lives in the data file: design asset → generated image (twin and its four looks for the phone,
  the try-on cards and "ask a friend"; wall people for the hero; feed posts for the feed tile and the in-phone
  feed; garments for the wardrobe rows and stylist). The design's `trench.png` cut-out tile uses `g-trench`,
  whose grey background matches the other tiles.
